# CV - Biel Bosch Ramo

This project is my personal CV website, created as a single page using HTML and CSS. It presents my education, technical skills, work experience and project ideas. The design is based on my [Figma CV](https://www.figma.com/design/7F7yf65uTZqpwHqKz33oat?node-id=1-10).

## Project analysis

### User profile (user persona)

**Name:** Laura Martínez (fictional user persona)  
**Age:** 30  
**Role:** Recruitment specialist at a small technology company  
**Location:** Girona, Spain  

Laura regularly reviews applications from students and people looking for their first opportunity in the technology sector. She usually has limited time to look at each CV, so she prefers information that is easy to find and understand. 
She wants to see what a candidate is studying, which programming languages and tools they know, whether they have work experience and what kinds of projects interest them. She also needs a clear way to contact the candidate.

For this user, the website should be simple, readable and well organised. It should give her a quick overview without requiring her to navigate through several pages or use complicated menus.

### Information architecture

I decided to organise the website as one page because I wanted it to work like a traditional CV. The visitor can see all the information by scrolling, without having to open different pages or use a navigation menu. This makes the website easy to understand and also keeps the HTML structure simple.

The page is divided into two main columns. On the left, I placed the information that helps visitors identify me quickly. It starts with my photograph, my name and a short introduction about my studies and interests. 
Below that, I added my technical skills, the languages I speak and my contact information. I chose this order because these details are useful at a glance and do not need long explanations.

The right column contains the main details of my CV. Education comes first because I am currently studying Computer Science and it is an important part of my profile. After that, I included my work experience, ordered from the most recent job to the oldest one. Each entry shows the year, job title, workplace, location and dates. Finally, I added a Projects section with four ideas I would like to develop. These are clearly marked as projects in planning rather than finished work.

I used clear section titles and kept related information together. This organisation allows visitors to read the whole CV in order or go directly to the part that interests them most.

### Visual design

For the visual design, I wanted the website to look like a simple, modern CV rather than a complicated portfolio. I used a vertical layout with two columns, similar to a printed document. The left column has a light beige background, while the main content area is white. This small difference in colour separates personal information from education, experience and projects without needing many boxes or decorative elements.

I chose dark text because it is easy to read on a light background. I used grey for secondary information, such as locations and dates, so that the most important words stand out. Orange is the accent colour. It appears in my surname and in the years of my work experience, giving the page a little personality without making it too colourful. The colours are defined as CSS variables, which makes them easy to change later.

I used the Inter font and different text sizes to create a clear order of importance. My name is larger than the section headings, and the headings are larger and bolder than the normal text. Thin lines separate the main sections, while spacing helps the page feel organised and not too crowded.

I also chose to avoid animations, large buttons and complicated visual effects. The photograph is the main visual element. Overall, I tried to keep the design professional but simple enough to build and understand using the HTML and CSS concepts from class.

## Project files

- `index.html`: the structure and content of the CV.
- `css/style.css`: the colours, typography, spacing and two-column layout.
- `assets/img/foto-biel.jpg`: my profile photograph.

## How to open the website

Download or extract the project folder and open `index.html` in a web browser. No installation, server or JavaScript is needed.
